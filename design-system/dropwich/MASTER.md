# Dropwich Design System

Updated September 2026 for the premium retail refinement.

## Direction

Dropwich uses playful premium retail minimalism: product-first imagery, balanced white space, a disciplined grid, clean sans-serif type, and restrained color. Modern lifestyle retailers inform browsing clarity only; every brand expression remains recognizably Dropwich.

## Core palette

- Ink: `#15110F`
- Dropwich orange: `#F04416`
- Orange hover: `#D93910`
- Sunny yellow: `#FFC94F`
- Warm cream: `#FFF9ED`
- Paper white: `#FFFDF8`
- Muted text: `#685E58`
- Border: `#E4DED6`

Orange is the primary action color. Yellow supports product imagery and highlights. Large surfaces stay white or warm cream. No unrelated accent colors are introduced.

## Typography

- Primary family: Manrope
- Display headings: Manrope 700-800, tight tracking, compact but readable line height
- Body: Manrope 400-600, at least 14px on compact UI and 16px on marketing content
- Fraunces is reserved for rare editorial accents, never the default page-heading font
- Marketing display type stays below 104px on desktop and scales fluidly on mobile

## Components

- Navigation is centered, open, and border-light, with separate search and account actions.
- Primary buttons use orange with white text and a consistent 12px radius.
- Cards use 1px warm-gray borders, 18-24px radii, and only a restrained hover shadow.
- Product cards give most of their area to consistent, contained imagery.
- Forms use visible labels, 52px fields, clear focus states, and inline feedback.
- Dashboard cards are white with thin borders; orange and yellow indicate emphasis without overwhelming the data.
- All routes share the same footer, navigation, focus ring, button language, and surface hierarchy.

## Layout

- Maximum content width: 1360px
- Marketing pages are spacious; dashboard density is moderate
- Desktop grids use 2-4 columns and collapse without horizontal overflow
- Mobile touch targets remain at least 44px
- Section spacing follows a consistent 8px-based rhythm

## Motion

- Use subtle 180-300ms interaction transitions and short reveal distances
- Motion explains hover, selection, drawer entry, or scroll arrival
- Avoid stamps, orbiting badges, exaggerated rotations, and offset black shadows
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Do not use

- Decorative section codes or forced microcopy
- Tiny uppercase captions that repeat a heading
- Dense archive styling, poster stamps, or Canva-like element clusters
- Heavy neo-brutalist shadows, glassmorphism, or unrelated gradients
- Animations that move layout or hide essential information
- Reference-brand colors, graphics, logos, or copied page compositions
