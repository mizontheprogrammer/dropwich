# Dropwich Design System

Updated September 2026 for the clean retail redesign.

## Direction

Dropwich uses playful retail minimalism: product-first imagery, generous white space, a simple grid, bold rounded sans-serif type, and restrained color. It may borrow the clarity of modern lifestyle retailers, but it must remain recognizably Dropwich.

## Core palette

- Ink: `#15110F`
- Dropwich orange: `#F04416`
- Sunny yellow: `#FFC94F`
- Warm cream: `#FFF9ED`
- Paper white: `#FFFDF8`
- Muted text: `#685E58`
- Border: `#DED7CF`

Orange is the primary action color. Yellow supports product imagery and highlights. Large surfaces stay white or warm cream.

## Typography

- Primary family: Manrope
- Display headings: Manrope 700–800, tight tracking, compact line height
- Body: Manrope 400–600, at least 14px on compact UI and 16px on marketing content
- Fraunces is reserved for rare editorial accents, never the default page-heading font

## Components

- Navigation is centered, open, and border-light. Active items use a filled ink pill or orange underline.
- Primary buttons use orange with white text and a full pill or 14–16px radius.
- Cards use 1px warm-gray borders, 20–28px radii, and no permanent heavy shadow.
- Product cards give most of their area to imagery.
- Forms use visible labels, 52px fields, and clear inline feedback.
- Dashboard cards are white with thin borders; orange and yellow indicate emphasis without overwhelming the data.

## Layout

- Maximum content width: 1480px
- Marketing pages are spacious; dashboard density is moderate
- Desktop grids use 2–4 columns and collapse without horizontal overflow
- Mobile touch targets remain at least 44px

## Motion

- Use subtle 200–420ms transitions and short reveal distances
- Motion should explain hover, selection, or scroll arrival
- Avoid stamps, orbiting badges, exaggerated rotations, and offset black shadows
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Do not use

- Decorative section codes or forced microcopy
- Tiny uppercase captions that repeat a heading
- Dense archive styling, poster stamps, or Canva-like element clusters
- Heavy neo-brutalist shadows
- Animations that move layout or hide essential information
